02Wiring: bin 1 shown

Bins 2 and 3 wire exactly the same. They share power, ground and the clock lines. Only the yellow-dashed lines are different for each one. Run everything off 3V3, not 5V.

BLK (backlight) is tied to 3V3 so the screen stays lit. Later it moves to a GPIO through a small transistor so the screens can dim.

04Mounting a scale (side view)

The bar measures by bending slightly. Bolt one end down and hang the platform off the other end, with spacers so the middle never touches anything.

05Before you power up

3V3 only

Power the HX711s and screens from the ESP32's 3V3 pin, not 5V. At 5V the HX711's data line can damage the ESP32.

Which ESP32 did you order?

These pins suit the standard ESP32 DevKit (WROOM, 30 or 38 pin). If yours is an S3, C3 or "Super Mini", tell me and I'll redo the pin map.

Solder the HX711 pins

The boards come with loose header pins. Solder them, or solder short leads straight on. Keep the load cell's own wires short and don't cut them.

Order the pin-header screen

For the bench, get the 8-pin module version. The bare 18 mm screen has a fine ribbon that needs an adapter. Save that for the real cabinet.

Arrow points down

Most bar cells have an arrow on the end. It should point the way the weight pushes. If readings go negative when you add weight, flip the cell or swap A+ and A−.

What a pass looks like

Each bin reads within 2–3 g of the calibration weights, holds steady over an hour, and counts 20 GPOs correctly as you add and remove them.
