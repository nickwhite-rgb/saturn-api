## PART 1 – CLIENT QUOTE (paste into AroFlo)

**14 Rose St Killarney – Electrical Works**

Thanks for the opportunity to quote. Below is the scope of works we've allowed for, based on your list and our site visit.

### Air conditioning
- Supply and install 2 x Mitsubishi Electric reverse cycle split systems (1 x 3.5kW, 1 x 2.5kW)
- Back-to-back installation (outdoor unit mounted directly behind the indoor unit on the external wall)
- New dedicated circuit and isolator for each unit

### Lighting
- Supply and install 3 x sensor downlights
- Supply and install 1 x Melelec 50W V4 LED floodlight
- Supply and install 1 x slim-profile downlight (to suit 28mm ceiling cavity)
- Install 23 x customer-supplied downlights, including a new plug base (socket outlet) for each light

### Smoke alarms
- Install 4 x hard-wired, interconnected photoelectric smoke alarms, compliant with QLD legislation
- *Price assumes the existing cabling (rough-in) is in place and serviceable. If new cabling is needed, we'll quote it as a variation before going ahead.*

### Power
- Supply and install 1 x additional power point under the house for the toilet (allowance of up to 10m cable run)
- Supply and fit off Clipsal Iconic (white) power points – 17 in total:
  - 4 x single GPO
  - 10 x double GPO
  - 1 x weatherproof double GPO
  - 2 x XA GPO

### Switches – Clipsal Iconic (white)
- 3 x 1-gang, 7 x 2-gang, 2 x 3-gang, 2 x 4-gang, 1 x 5-gang (15 switch plates in total)

### Bathroom
- Install customer-supplied 3-in-1 bathroom unit (exhaust, heat and light)

### Ceiling fans
- Install customer-supplied ceiling fans – **priced per fan**. Final number to be confirmed by you.

### Kitchen – make safe
- The existing kitchen cabling will be isolated and terminated in compliant junction boxes, ready for the future kitchen fit-out. Kitchen fit-off will be quoted separately when you're ready.

### TV
- Terminate and fit off existing TV cabling to TV wall points so the points are usable

### Exclusions
- Dimmers (can be added – ask us for a price)
- TV antenna supply, installation or alignment
- Kitchen fit-off (quoted separately later)
- Supply of the downlights, bathroom unit and fans you're providing
- Ceiling fan bracing or timber noggins, if none is in place (quoted on the spot if needed)
- Any rectification of existing faults found during the works
- Switchboard upgrade, if one is needed to support the new circuits (we'll advise before starting)

### Notes
- All work is done by licensed electricians and comes with a Certificate of Testing & Compliance
- Quote valid for 30 days

### Site notes (from Nick's walk-through)
- **A/C:** both units go back-to-back. If either can't go back-to-back, call Nick or the office before starting, because it changes the price.
- **Smoke alarms:** the client *thinks* the rough-in is done. Check it first. If cable is missing, stop and raise a variation in AroFlo.
- **Slim downlight:** the cavity is only 28mm. Make sure the fitting suits it before you go to site.
- **Under-house GPO (toilet):** about 10m run. Access is under the house, coming off the post.
- **23 client downlights:** fit a **Clipsal 413 socket outlet (plug base)** for every light. That's 23 x 413s on the materials list.
- **Fans:** the client still has to confirm how many (they're getting a price from Haymans). Check the ceiling has a fixing point or timber, because **no timber was noted** at the walk-through.
- **Kitchen:** make safe only. Terminate in JBs and label at the board as "Kitchen – future".
- **TV:** fit off the points only. **No antenna work.** That's the client's problem.
- **Dimmers:** NOT included. Standard switches only.
